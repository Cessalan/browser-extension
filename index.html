Confi: polish sensitive text without it ever leaving your computer

Confi reviews, corrects and formats your emails, notes and reports with an AI that runs entirely on your computer. Made for text about patients, clients or employees. Nothing is ever sent online.

What it does

  • Checks your text and suggests what to fix, or rewrites it clearer, shorter or more professional.
  • Corrects spelling and grammar, and keeps your text in the language you wrote it in: English or French.
  • Spots file numbers, social insurance and health insurance numbers, dates of birth, phone numbers and email addresses before you share.
  • Lets you dictate your text by voice, transcribed on your computer.

Who it is for

Healthcare (liaison notes, referrals, emails to physicians), legal (client letters, case notes), human resources (employee files, feedback) and social services (case reports, client files).

Private by design

Confi downloads its AI once, then runs it right in your browser. Your text is never sent to a server. No account, no cookies, no analytics, and it works offline after the first setup.
